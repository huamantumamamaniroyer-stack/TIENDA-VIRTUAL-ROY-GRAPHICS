== Description ==

Automotive Car Dealer Theme is designed around vehicle listings, dealership information, and automotive business content. Its layout provides dedicated sections for presenting vehicles with details such as images, specifications, pricing, and descriptions. A structured homepage can display featured vehicles, service information, company details, and contact options in an organized format. The Automotive Car Dealer theme supports responsive layouts, allowing pages and vehicle listings to adapt to different screen sizes. Customization options can be used to adjust colors, typography, layouts, and other visual elements without changing the core theme files. Navigation menus help organize vehicle categories and important business pages for visitors. Vehicle-focused content can include individual listing pages with photographs, descriptions, technical information, and pricing details. Call-to-action sections can direct visitors toward inquiries or contact forms. The design can also accommodate blog posts, testimonials, service details, and other supporting content. Its clean page structure provides a practical foundation for presenting automotive inventory and related business information while keeping key content accessible across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

== Installation ==

1. In your admin panel, go to Appearance > Themes and click the Add New button.
2. Click Upload Theme and Choose File, then select the theme's .zip file. Click Install Now.
3. Click Activate to use your new theme right away.
